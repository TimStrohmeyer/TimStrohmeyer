# Hi, I'm Tim 👋

**Ph.D. student at [ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/en.html) · Pre-Doctoral Researcher at [IBM Research – Zurich](https://research.ibm.com/labs/zurich)**

I work on **multimodal machine learning for document understanding** — teaching vision-language models to read documents the way domain experts do: layout, tables, formulas, and chemical structures included.

At ETH I am part of the [Computer Vision Lab](https://vision.ee.ethz.ch) led by [Prof. Dr. Ender Konukoglu](https://people.ee.ethz.ch/~kender/). At IBM Research I work with [Dr. Peter W. J. Staar](https://research.ibm.com/people/peter-staar) in the *AI for Knowledge* group, as part of the team behind [Docling](https://github.com/docling-project/docling). I hold an M.Sc. in Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence from TU Munich.

---

## 🔬 Research interests

`Computer Vision` · `Multimodal Machine Learning` · `Vision-Language Models` · `Document AI` · `OCR` · `Optical Chemical Structure Recognition`

Recurring themes in my work:

- **End-to-end document parsing** — replacing brittle multi-stage pipelines with single multimodal models.
- **Structured output from pixels** — predicting graphs, tables, and machine-readable representations rather than plain text.
- **Scientific & patent literature at scale** — chemical structures, Markush formulas, and key-value extraction from real-world documents.
- **Synthetic data & evaluation** — building the datasets and benchmarks these models need to be trusted.

## 🚧 What I'm working on

- **[MarkushGrapher-2](https://timstrohmeyer.github.io/MarkushGrapher-2-website/)** — end-to-end multimodal recognition of chemical structures, including Markush (generic) structures from patents. *CVPR 2026, first author.* → [model](https://huggingface.co/docling-project/MarkushGrapher-2) · [datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets/docling-project/MarkushGrapher-2-Datasets) · [code](https://github.com/DS4SD/MarkushGrapher)
- **[Docling](https://github.com/docling-project/docling)** — open-source document conversion used across the ML ecosystem. I contribute on the vision-language and chemistry side, plus layout analysis models and evaluation ([docling-metrics](https://github.com/docling-project/docling-metrics)).
- **Key-value & information extraction** — end-to-end extraction of structured fields from document images (ICDAR 2026).
- **Ongoing Ph.D. research** — pushing VLMs toward reliable, verifiable understanding of scientific documents.
